# 「그리스도」 (christ) — English native review

Reviewer: native English, evangelical/Reformed perspective. Report only — `motion/films/christ/i18n/en.json` was not edited.
Sources: `motion/films/christ/script.json` (Korean) · `motion/films/christ/i18n/en.json` (English, ESV).
Audience: Christians 55+ who don't know the Bible well, many on TV; text shows one or two short lines at a time; the same English is narrated.

**Totals: HIGH 0 · MED 6 · LOW 13.** No line is theologically wrong. The MED items are about clarity when heard, grammar, one
meaning drift (N15) and lines too long for their slot.

Timing note: slot lengths below come from the Korean `at` values (start of the next line). If the English voice track is retimed
on its own, the length flags (N02, N20, K3) matter less. Rough pace for calm narration: about 2.5 words per second.

Checker note (`motion/films/check-quotes.mjs`): the text is split on `/` and `…` and each piece must appear in the verse. `voice`
must be one unbroken stretch of the verse. Matching ignores case and quote marks. All quote suggestions below pass this check.

## Theology summary
- **"Christ is not a last name" (N03, N17):** sound. It is a title meaning Messiah/Anointed One, and N17 turns it into a confession,
  so the framing doesn't suggest the word is unimportant. Optional "a title" in N03.
- **N15:** fix the drift. The point is that a *suffering* Messiah was the foretold **road** (Luke 24:26). It is not here meant as
  "he is the way".
- **N16 "three offices, in one Person":** correct Reformed threefold office; "Person" is the right Christological word. No change
  needed.
- **N20:** drop "and then", so the line doesn't make Acts 10 sound as if it came right after the resurrection.
- Nothing in the English brings in a different religious register. The terms match ESV throughout (Messiah, Christ, anoint,
  Holy Spirit, garments/tunic, cast lots).
