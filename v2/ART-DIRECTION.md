# Production art prompts

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production background artwork for a mobile-first biblical scroll film. Create one single PORTRAIT image, 2:3 ratio, full bleed, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO UI, NO border, NO watermark. Style: exquisite cinematic matte painting with visible painterly brushwork, sophisticated atmospheric perspective, tactile stone and water, immense scale, restrained luminous light, richly detailed but readable silhouettes; a serious beautifully illustrated adventure film, not flat vector, not geometric canvas art, not plastic 3D. Compose the meaningful action in central 70 percent width, leave the lower 20 percent darker and quieter for website text overlay. Visual consistency: deep ink/navy shadows, pearl highlights, warm amber accents, realistic ancient Near Eastern world interpreted poetically. No depiction of God. 

## creation

A breathtaking primordial garden valley at blue-hour dawn. A river winds from the distant radiant horizon toward the foreground through lush cedar trees and waterfalls. Two tiny fully clothed human figures stand together near the river, seen from behind, dwarfed by immense landscape. A warm shaft of light suggests harmony and presence without depicting God.

## fall

Outside a monumental natural stone doorway in an ancient lush garden, two tiny fully clothed travelers step away from the warm garden into a vast lonely twilight wilderness. The garden gate behind them glows faintly while foreground is cold plum and copper. Strong readable symbolism: relationship broken, home left behind. No apple icon, no scary monster.

## patriarch

Abraham, a tiny elderly robed traveler seen from behind, stands on a desert ridge beside a small tent and gazes up into an immense sky of thousands of stars and the Milky Way. Vast indigo sky, amber campfire, flowing ochre sand dunes. The stars are abundant but natural; a thin warm ribbon-like caravan path crosses the lower desert.

## exodus

The Exodus: a narrow dry path leading between two monumental vertical walls of turquoise seawater, a tiny crowd of robed Israelites walking away from camera toward a warm distant pillar of fire. Camera at human eye level so walls tower overhead. Water has luminous sculpted foam, visible depth and frothy edges, ground is rough warm ochre. A spectacular readable path of rescue, no violence.

## kingdom

Ancient Jerusalem on a terraced sunlit hill, historically inspired limestone flat-roofed houses and a monumental ancient Israelite temple without domes or minarets. A winding path climbs through olive groves toward a luminous golden city. Late sunset volumetric light and deep violet shadows. The subject is a kingdom full of hope, monumental yet intimate.

## exile

Exile from ancient Jerusalem: small group of robed travelers walking away from a broken stone city gate across a cold windswept landscape. Crumbling terraces, distant smoke and muted blue storm clouds; no gore, no battle, no modern city. The ancient place of home stands ruined behind them; strong emotional composition of loss and longing.

## bethlehem

Bethlehem at night: humble first-century Judean limestone houses across a hillside. In the foreground right a low stable doorway spills extraordinary warm lamplight onto a winding stone path. One radiant star high above and a tiny robed family visible only as modest silhouettes inside the doorway. Indigo night, pearl starlight, tactile warm stone; a great promise arrives in a small place. No baby close-up, no European church, no halo icon.

## cross

A lone rough timber cross silhouetted on a rocky hill at dusk under immense storm clouds breaking with a thin sliver of pale light. Very small against the sky, solemn absence not graphic suffering. Desaturated ink, warm ash and muted burgundy. No person on cross, no gore, no lightning spectacle. Foreground winding path leads towards cross. Cinematic restrained silence.

## resurrection

View from inside a rough first-century rock-cut tomb looking out toward a luminous dawn garden. The round stone has been rolled aside on the left, linen folded on a stone bench. Brilliant warm morning light streams through dust, olive trees and flowers outside, blue shadows inside. No person depicted, no cross, no writing. A deeply emotional transition from darkness into life.

## church

A first-century Mediterranean harbor at luminous teal dawn. Small wooden sailing boats, ancient limestone streets with little groups of ordinary robed people carrying warm lamps; the lit paths branch outward from the harbor toward distant islands. A spreading message carried by people, no fantasy flying lanterns, no modern church buildings. Painterly amber lights in cool teal landscape.

## restoration

A magnificent renewed world: a luminous stone city integrated into a living garden, a broad clear river flowing through terraces of trees and flowers toward the viewer, waterfalls, radiant pearl sky. Architecture recalls ancient limestone arcades not European cathedral, no domes, no futuristic skyscrapers. Tiny human figures freely gather beside the river. No visible sun disc, the whole city gently illuminated from within. A powerful visual rhyme with a primordial garden valley, home restored.
